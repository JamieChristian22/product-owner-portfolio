# 📌 Executive Summary

This project delivers a centralized **Executive KPI Dashboard** for a multi-store retail environment.

As Product Owner, I designed a governance framework to improve:

- Revenue visibility
- Margin performance
- Labor efficiency
- Shrink reduction
- Inventory turnover

The goal was to reduce decision latency and enable leadership to act on performance drivers quickly.

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

Provide executive-level visibility into:

- Sales performance by region
- Store-level margin trends
- Sales per labor hour
- Inventory turnover
- Shrink & operational leakage

---

# 📊 Problem Statement

Retail leadership lacked:

- Unified multi-store visibility
- Standardized KPI definitions
- Real-time performance benchmarking
- Clear linkage between labor cost and revenue
- Structured shrink monitoring

As a result:
- Margin erosion went unnoticed
- Labor costs were misaligned with revenue
- High-performing stores were not benchmarked effectively

# 🧠 Product Strategy

### Strategic Initiatives

1️⃣ Standardize KPI definitions across stores  
2️⃣ Create executive roll-up dashboard  
3️⃣ Introduce store performance benchmarking  
4️⃣ Identify high-shrink locations  
5️⃣ Launch labor efficiency optimization initiative
